Persisted/exported coordinates are from the city directory, Geoapify or a user-confirmed pin. Google search content is rendered by the official component and is not copied to the persistent itinerary. Transit results are transient and shown without a map; users add their own ticket details. OpenStreetMap, Geoapify and OpenMapTiles attribution must remain visible in the map and generated exports.
